Implication for AISNE

Both challenges demand the same response: regional data infrastructure that helps 275 member schools make decisions collectively, rather than navigating enrollment pressure and AI governance in isolation. Expanded Pulse Points benchmarking, model AI policies, and disaggregated enrollment projections would turn AISNE's convening role into a strategic advantage for every member school.
